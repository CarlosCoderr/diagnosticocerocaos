# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (chosen by the user).

## Users

Lucía: an expert or personal-brand owner (consultant, coach, program seller) who already has an audience and already gets messages, but loses people after they write. Her month-to-month revenue swings, her WhatsApp has cold conversations, she has tried ads or a cheap freelancer and concluded "ads don't work", and she owns a pretty site that sells nothing. She arrives from social content and reads in Spanish (Peru; prices in soles).

## Product Purpose

A long sales page built around a ~10 minute VSL for **Diagnóstico Cero Caos**, a paid 60–90 minute session with Carlos Coder plus a written document (brand/avatar/pain analysis, defined offer, recommended route). It sells only step 1 of the four-step Método Cero Caos (diagnosis → infrastructure → training → ads on day 15). Success is the visitor tapping the single CTA: a WhatsApp message to Carlos with her name and what she sells, then booking and paying for the session.

## Positioning

One person does the landing, automation, training and ads, and stays on to operate and train instead of delivering a file and disappearing. Ads go last (day 15), never without a diagnosis, "aunque me lo pidas, aunque me pagues". The diagnostic is deliberately paid to filter out people who only ask the price.

## Operating Context

Sources of truth, in the project root: `PÁGINA DE VENTA — DIAGNÓSTICO CERO CAOS.md` (full page copy, 12 sections), `VSL — DIAGNÓSTICO CERO CAOS.md` (video script), `Estructura en Bloques de la Landing Page …md` (12-block order; latest, states price S/ 297). Voice: first person, direct, Peruvian Spanish, short punchy closers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single CTA: WhatsApp, with a preloaded message (text in the copy doc).
- Everything in soles. One real case only, never presented as a guarantee. No false urgency, countdowns, scarcity, struck-through "values", bonuses, testimonials or the word "embudo".
- Visible results disclaimer and Terms/Privacy links are required (Meta rejects landings without them).
- Price: S/ 297 (blocks doc); earlier copy said a S/ 197–297 range. Open: the guarantee (no guarantee is shown until the user decides), the payment method, the WhatsApp number, the final legal URLs.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Carlos Coder (personal brand, not an agency name).
- The user pinned the visual language through seven reference screenshots in the project root (clean white pages, oversized tight grotesk headlines with serif-italic accents, one tactile hero object, hairline-framed grids, pill buttons, a receipt/ticket card). That pin lives in the surface brief and DESIGN.md.

## Evidence on Hand

- One real case: a marketing agency selling to aesthetic centers. S/ 300 ad spend in week 1, 11 messages, 1 qualified client paid S/ 3,000, kept on monthly retention, later added Carlos to their team.
- Pending from the user, not to be invented: the Meta panel capture, the WhatsApp closing capture, the owner's video (`[VIDEO DUEÑA]`), the VSL video file, Carlos's photo (current profile photo not validated), payment details, legal URLs. Absent until supplied; use marked placeholders.

## Product Principles

- One decision on the page: write to Carlos on WhatsApp.
- Honesty is the differentiator: one case labelled as one case, a "who it's not for" filter, no promised numbers.
- The user's copy is the source of truth; design serves it. Changes to wording are stated, never silent.
- The caos is the villain; order (a system, a route, a document) is the promise.
